# MMD acquisition + recruitment execution — October 2026

This document restores the growth work that was intentionally paused while Payments, Kenji and Studio were being repaired.

## Two separate funnels

### 1. New customers -> MY MMD

Customer acquisition must land in the customer/member world, not MMD APP.

Primary path:

`Ad / social / Telegram Preview -> relevant public MMD page -> /member/login or verified MY MMD route -> MY MMD`

Use short customer-facing copy. The acquisition surface may introduce membership, CARE BACK, current Model/editorial updates or a service path, but the final customer state remains backend-authoritative.

### 2. Workers / Models -> MMD APP

Recruitment must land in the worker/model world.

Primary path:

`Job/recruitment post -> short Job Board link -> job-first entry -> LINE/MMD APP verification -> exact Job Board / MMD APP destination`

Do not send worker recruitment traffic into MY MMD.

## Channel work to execute

- Rotate Travel Model/editorial discovery into Telegram Preview when public-safe material is available.
- Keep X / Instagram recruitment creative separate from customer acquisition creative.
- Use LINE/customer channels for customer conversion, not worker recruitment.
- Build a lightweight PR/campaign-operator sourcing lane so campaign publishing does not depend entirely on Per.
- Every paid/always-on campaign must inherit the APP/SIGIL release gate before launch.

## PR / campaign operator scope

The role being sourced should be operational, not a new source of business truth. Responsibilities may include:

- prepare and schedule approved posts;
- maintain a simple content calendar;
- collect approved public-safe assets;
- route drafts back to Per for final approval;
- record publication links and campaign results;
- never change pricing, entitlement, availability, payment truth or Model privacy rules.

## Weekly review

At least once per week, record:

- which customer acquisition posts actually ran;
- which recruitment posts actually ran;
- which funnel destination each post used;
- whether the destination smoke passed;
- which posts generated qualified customer/worker actions;
- what is blocked by creative, media, app acceptance or owner review.

No private client/model data or confidential commercial numbers belong in this public repository.
