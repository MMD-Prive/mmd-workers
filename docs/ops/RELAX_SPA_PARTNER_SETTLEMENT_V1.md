# Relax Spa by 9: referrals, receipts and agreement-based shares

Implemented presentation and a durable operational ledger. This document is not
evidence of deployment, real receipts, spa acceptance or transfers.

## Pages and ownership

- MY Partner: `/partner/dashboard/relax-spa?t=<existing-partner-token>`, linked
  from the current Partner dashboard. Uses the existing signature, stored hash,
  Active and Recognized checks. Token and cookies are never forwarded to MMS.
- MMS Partner / Owner: `/internal/admin/mms/relax-spa`, linked from the existing
  MMS overview. Requires the signed credential-bound browser session.
- Four views: referrals, agreements, services/receipts, shares/payout evidence.
- Public Relax Spa marketing pages and the customer MY MMS app keep their routes.

`partners-worker` verifies identity and passes `partner:<Airtable-record-id>`.
The MMS admin gateway passes the verified MMS Partner account or Owner actor.
Browser actor/role headers never grant authority. Partners can create referrals
and read only their own recommendations and partner payouts; they cannot accept
on behalf of the spa, approve financial agreements, receipts or disbursements.

## Operational steps

1. Partner selects an existing Active MMS Therapist and records a recommendation.
   New Therapists first use the existing MMS application/review process.
2. Owner records the spa's acceptance or refusal with an evidence reference.
3. Owner approves an immutable agreement with a terms reference, explicit basis
   (`received_revenue` or `net_profit`) and Therapist / spa / referring Partner /
   Male Massage percentages totalling 100%. There is no default rate. Create a
   new agreement for changed terms; existing service snapshots remain frozen.
4. Owner links an existing canonical MMS Job assigned to the same Therapist,
   records evidence that the job belongs to the spa, the expected service amount,
   and itemized approved direct costs with references. This ledger never creates,
   dispatches, starts or completes another Job. Use the existing MMS job tools.
5. Owner records each actual receipt after inspecting its amount, recipient,
   timestamp and unique transaction/evidence reference. Partial receipts are
   supported. This is `owner_manual_evidence_review`, not automatic bank/slip
   verification and not a mutation of the global Payments table.
6. Settlement re-reads the exact canonical Job and requires Completed, the same
   Therapist, no test flag, and full reviewed receipts. Costs are deducted only
   for a net-profit agreement. Do not include an expense already represented by
   a share (for example the Therapist's payout) as a second cost.
7. Owner records each actual recipient payout with its exact approved amount,
   recipient, time and unique evidence reference. This records inspected transfer
   evidence; it does not execute a bank transfer. A zero share needs no payout.

All money is integer satang. Largest-remainder allocation keeps the four shares
equal to the distributable amount. Direct costs are separately itemized; expense
reimbursement is not automatically generated by this module. The spa's intake
approval and each actual payment remain manual evidenced operations.

## Persistence and limits

`RELAX_SPA_LEDGER` is a SQLite-backed Durable Object in mms-worker. One object
serializes this spa's writes in storage transactions. Scoped request keys,
payload fingerprints, unique evidence checks, and unique canonical Job links
prevent retry credits and duplicate payouts. An append-only action trail records
the verified actor and time. Failed transactions do not retain partial decisions.
Same-origin JSON POST is required at both browser gateways.

The first release is bounded to 200 referrals, 200 agreements, 200 service links
and 5,000 actions; further writes require an archive migration. Catalog reads
fail if they cannot return the full active roster within 100 items. Storage errors
show unavailable, never a fake empty or paid state. Cancelled/expired/test Jobs
cannot be linked; incomplete or subsequently cancelled Jobs cannot be paid out.
Receipts and payouts cannot be edited or deleted. Overpayments, refunds, reversals,
disputes and amendments require separate reviewed reconciliation; this version
fails closed rather than overwriting an approved settlement.

This is a dedicated spa ledger, not another materializer for existing Model
Partner Commissions. Do not book the same earning through both lanes. Receipt
references must identify actual transactions; batch transfers across recipients
need itemized evidence identifying each payout, not reuse of one reference.

## Rollout

1. Review and merge, then deploy mms-worker with the new Durable Object migration.
2. Deploy partners-worker with its MMS_WORKER binding, then admin-worker.
3. Verify the Owner page, a recognized MY Partner's page and an MMS Partner page
   on mobile; confirm another Partner cannot see the first Partner's records.
4. Use an actual accepted referral and real non-test MMS Job. Verify partial/full
   receipts, Completed gating, all approved amounts and one inspected payout.
5. Inspect the audit trail and duplicate retries. No production payment or transfer
   is authorized merely by deployment of this change.

Tests: MMS runtime suite; Relax Spa browser gateway and ledger tests; Partner
suite (including verified token and finance-role boundary); Wrangler dry runs.
