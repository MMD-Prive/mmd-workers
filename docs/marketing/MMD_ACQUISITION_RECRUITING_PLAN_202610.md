# MMD Acquisition + Recruiting Operating Plan — Oct 2026

Objective: run customer acquisition and Model/workforce recruiting in parallel without mixing the two funnels.

## Customer lane

Destination: **MY MMD / canonical customer path**.

Weekly output:
- 2 public-world customer creatives;
- 1 Telegram Preview highlight;
- 1 CARE BACK / trust / continuity post;
- 1 model/service discovery creative only when destination/search media is production-ready.

Primary channels:
- LINE OA;
- X;
- Telegram Preview;
- selected paid social after the APP/SIGIL release gate passes.

Do not send new customers into internal admin, Model APP, or private Model onboarding.

## Model / worker lane

Destination: **MMD APP / canonical recruitment or Job Board path**.

Weekly output:
- 2 recruitment posts;
- 1 Job Board opportunity round-up;
- 1 Travel / Extreme / Public talent call where demand exists;
- direct follow-up for qualified applicants.

Entry rule:

```text
Job / recruitment post
-> short link
-> MMD APP Welcome
-> required onboarding fields
-> LINE Verify
-> identity resolution
-> Job Board / originating intent
```

## PR / campaign operator search

Need one operating owner who can:
- prepare and schedule approved creatives;
- keep channel calendar;
- route leads to the correct funnel;
- record post/ad references and spend;
- never edit membership/payment/model truth.

Candidate sources:
- existing creative / social contacts;
- model referrals;
- freelance social/PR operators;
- small Bangkok lifestyle/creator agencies.

First screening:
- mobile-first social campaign samples;
- experience with LINE / X / Telegram/community channels;
- ability to work from locked brand templates;
- reporting discipline;
- availability for a 30-day pilot.

## 30-day pilot scorecard

Track:
- posts delivered;
- destination opens;
- verified customer entries;
- verified Model applications;
- qualified leads;
- spend;
- cost per verified entry/application.

Do not optimize on raw message volume alone.

## Owner cadence

Monday: choose weekly offers/jobs/creative.
Wednesday: review destination/lead quality.
Sunday: keep / stop / revise channels based on verified outcomes.
